# Product consistency

Research on keeping a product the same across a generative design workflow, and on measuring it the way a person sees it: colour, shape, parts, text and logo, what goes missing and what gets invented.

Follows [From CAD to Shelf](https://github.com/meryboth/from-cad-to-shelf). Its products (LUMEN, FIELD 16) and passes are the starting point.

> Status: literature review and experiment design. No runs yet.
